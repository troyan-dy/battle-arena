# Style passport — Пиксельная арена
View: 1920×1190 (the reference arena 947×587 × 2.027), high 3/4 top-down; HD pixel (sprites on a ~2 ref-px / ~4 view-px grid, smooth UI type, smooth glows and bloom) → full-resolution pipeline, linear + mipmaps, no frame pixelation.
Game family: horde survival / auto-shooter (Vampire Survivors-like).   Style family: HD pixel, dark fantasy.
Value: low-key, mean L* 15, p5/p50/p95 = 2/7/53; the light pools (cyan rune ring under the hero, warm torches, white-blue ice FX) carry the highs.
Light: cool moonlight from the upper left; painted light = additive glows (ice FX, torches, staff gem); bloom on FX only.
Palette: R1 neutral #030911..#d4e3e7; slate stone #2a3036–#4d5a5c; moss #1a2e23 #2a3e26 #41a587; ice #73c2e7; violet #5137a9; gold #f9c32d; ember #e97724. Shadows cool blue-violet, lights warm yellow-green.
Line: 1 px near-black #030911 outline on actors and props (~1.1 ref px); the hero gets an extra code ink pass (L.inked r 1.6).
Shading: 3–4 flat tones per material, hard terminator, hue-shifted ramps.
Shapes: chibi proportions (head ≈ 1/3), chunky silhouettes; enemies blue-grey with red/orange eyes.
Detail: faces, eyes, weapons; ground is a quiet mossy flagstone field.
Atmosphere: no fog bands; slow dark macro shade drifting over the ground; vignette.
UI: dark navy rounded panels with thin grey-blue borders; condensed sans type (white / pale blue); red HP bar, blue XP bar; white chunky damage numbers with a dark outline.
